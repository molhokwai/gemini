# Foundation Frameworks: Core Psychology Curriculum

## [ ] Module 1: Foundational Frameworks
### 1. Yale University: Introduction to Psychology (PSYC 110)
*   **Focus:** Subconscious drives, evolutionary behavioral biology, personality traits, and social dynamics.
*   **Core Reading Assignments:**
    *   *Grey, Peter.* "Psychology" (Selected Introductory Chapters).
    *   *Nettle, Daniel.* "Personality: What makes you the way you are" (Chapters 1-3).
    *   *Schacter, D. L., Gilbert, D. T., & Wegner, D. M.* "Psychology" (Foundational Models).
*   **Specific Exam Structure:**
    *   **Midterm Assessment:** 50 Multiple Choice questions + 3 short-answer conceptual applications evaluating foundational history, evolution, and developmental theories.
    *   **Final Comprehensive Examination:** 75 Multiple Choice questions + 2 comprehensive essay analyses mapping clinical pathologies and social psychology theories to real-world behavioral scenarios.

### 2. MIT: Introduction to Psychology (9.00SC)
*   **Focus:** Brain anatomy, neural transmission, sensory mechanics, memory architectures, and empirical research engineering.
*   **Core Reading Assignments:**
    *   *Gazzaniga, Michael.* "Psychological Science" (Chapters on Neuroscience, Sensation, and Cognitive Systems).
    *   *Sacks, Oliver.* "The Man Who Mistook His Wife for a Hat" (Selected clinical neuropsychology case studies).
*   **Specific Exam Structure:**
    *   **Unit 1 Exam:** Biological Foundations, Neuroscience, and Sensation/Perception mechanics.
    *   **Unit 2 Exam:** Learning Systems, Cognition, Memory Architectures, and Developmental milestones.
    *   **Unit 3 Exam:** Social Influence, Personality Variations, Psychopathology, and Clinical Interventions.
    *   **Final Cumulative Assessment:** Rigorous evaluation covering all 3 academic pillars with advanced research design questions.
